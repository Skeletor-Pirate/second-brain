# 🛡️ Area: Knowledge Management (PKM)

## 📌 Principles
1. **Capture**: Keep friction low using `00_Inbox`.
2. **Organize**: Categorize into `Projects`, `Areas`, `Resources`, or `Archive`.
3. **Distill**: Summarize using progressive summarization and wikilinks.
4. **Express**: Produce output (code, articles, projects) from stored insights.
